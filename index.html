Three fields, one engineering community

SEDECH’27 brings together researchers and engineers working on intelligent energy systems, advanced and optimal control, and biomedical signal and image processing. The common thread is artificial intelligence and optimization applied to real engineering problems.

The conference is organised by the ERTE team of the STIS research centre at ENSAM Rabat, Mohammed V University in Rabat. It welcomes original research papers, review papers and industrial case studies.

2 days

Keynotes, parallel oral sessions, poster sessions and a PhD forum.

3 tracks + 1

Three thematic tracks and a cross-cutting AI and digital twins track.

Indexed proceedings

Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for publication in indexed proceedings.

Special issues

Extended versions of selected papers invited to partner journals.

Call for papers

Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished work in the following tracks. Topics include, but are not limited to:

Track 1

Energy and optimization

  • Renewable energy forecasting (PV, wind) with AI and generative models
  • Smart grids, microgrids and energy management
  • Energy storage, batteries and hydrogen
  • Power electronics for renewable integration
  • Metaheuristics and multi-objective optimization
  • Energy efficiency in buildings and industry
Track 2

Control and optimal control

  • Optimal control and model predictive control (MPC)
  • Robust, adaptive and nonlinear control
  • Control of power converters and electrical drives
  • Robotics, micro- and nano-robotics
  • Learning-based and data-driven control
  • Cyber-physical and networked systems
Track 3

Biomedical signal and image processing

  • EEG, ECG and EMG analysis
  • Maternal and fetal ECG (MECG, FECG) extraction
  • Blind source separation and denoising
  • Medical image processing and analysis
  • AI-assisted diagnosis and decision support
  • Wearable devices and e-health
Cross-cutting track: AI and digital twinsMachine learning, generative AI, explainable AI and digital twins applied to any of the three fields.

Keynote speakers

Confirmed invited speakers. More names will be announced soon.

Prof. Youssef Agrebi Zorgani

Higher Institute of Technological Studies of Sfax, Tunisia

Track 2 · Control and optimal control

The Self-Aware Motor Drive: Fully Adaptive Sensorless Control with Real-Time Parameter and Torque Estimation for Industry 4.0

Biography

Dr. Youssef Agrebi Zorgani is a Professor of Power Electronics and Electrical Drives in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the Higher Institute of Technological Studies of Sfax, Tunisia.

He holds a dual Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the National School of Engineers of Sfax (ENIS, Tunisia) and in Automatic Control from Aix-Marseille University (France). He is the author of over 40 scientific publications and one book. His research at the Lab-STA laboratory (ENIS) covers electrical machine control, power electronics, renewable energy and control systems.

He reviews for several journals and conferences, including IEEE ones, and chaired the Organizing Committee of the ECONOV 2022 International Conference on Ecological Innovation. He has been an invited speaker at Gdansk University of Technology (Poland, twice) and the University of Guelma (Algeria).

Dr. Paolo R. Massenio

Department of Electrical and Information Engineering, Politecnico di Bari, Italy

Track 2 · Control and optimal control

Talk title to be announced

Biography

Paolo Roberto Massenio received the M.Sc. (Hons.) and Ph.D. degrees in Control Engineering from the Polytechnic University of Bari, Italy, in 2017 and 2021. He was a Visiting Scholar at the University of Texas at Arlington, USA, from 2019 to 2020, and in 2023 he was awarded a Mercator Fellowship by the German Research Foundation (DFG) to carry out research in soft robotics at Saarland University, Germany.

He is currently an Assistant Professor of Automatic Control at the Polytechnic University of Bari, where he coordinates several European and national projects in clean aviation propulsion, defense and industrial automation. His research interests include control of smart material devices, soft robotics, distributed control, control and identification of electric machines, and reinforcement learning-based algorithms.

Dr. Massenio has authored over 30 scientific publications and holds one patent. He received Best Paper Awards from the IEEE Power and Energy Society in 2021 and 2022. He serves as an Associate Editor for Control Engineering Practice and as a member of the Conference Editorial Board of the IEEE Control Systems Society.

Prof. Mohamed Bakhouya

International University of Rabat, Morocco

Cross-cutting · Distributed and adaptive systems

Talk title to be announced

Biography

Mohamed Bakhouya is a Professor of Computer Science at the International University of Rabat. He received his HDR from the Université de Haute-Alsace (UHA, France) in 2013 and his Ph.D. from the Université de Technologie de Belfort-Montbéliard (UTBM, France) in 2005. He has more than ten years of experience in participating in and coordinating sponsored projects.

He has evaluated research projects for the French National Research Agency (ANR, 2011), the Italian Ministry of Education, University and Research (2012, 2013, 2016, 2017), the Qatar National Research Fund (2019, 2020), the European Commission FP7 (2013–2015), CHIST-ERA (2021–2022) and CNRST (2022–2024).

He was an editor of the journal IJARAS and has served as guest editor of several international journals, including ACM Transactions on Autonomous and Adaptive Systems, Concurrency and Computation: Practice and Experience, FGCS and MICRO. His research covers the design, validation and implementation of distributed and adaptive systems, architectures and protocols.

Prof. Myriam Tami

MICS laboratory, Université Paris-Saclay and CentraleSupélec, France

Cross-cutting · Artificial intelligence

Talk title to be announced

Biography

Myriam Tami is an Associate Professor (HDR) in Artificial Intelligence, specializing in machine learning and statistics, at the Mathematics Interacting with Computer Science (MICS) laboratory of Université Paris-Saclay and CentraleSupélec. She leads or takes part in several publicly funded and industrial research projects, including the ANR JCJC project CLearDeep, co-leads the MSc in AI at CentraleSupélec and teaches several machine learning courses.

Before joining CentraleSupélec, she was a postdoctoral researcher in machine learning at the LIG laboratory, Université Grenoble Alpes, in collaboration with TotalEnergies. She received her Ph.D. in Biostatistics from the University of Montpellier in 2016.

Her research focuses on reliable and trustworthy statistical learning at the intersection of machine learning, statistics and causality: interpretable and knowledge-driven models, gray-box AI, and the fusion of deep learning and causality, including causal representation learning, counterfactual robustness and causal discovery.

Submission guidelines

  1. Prepare a full paper of 8 to 12 pages in the conference template. Abstracts and short papers are not accepted.
  2. Create a free Microsoft CMT account, then submit the PDF on the SEDECH’27 CMT page, choosing one track.
  3. Each paper is reviewed by at least two members of the scientific committee (single-blind).
  4. At least one author must register and present the paper in person for it to appear in the proceedings.

Publication

Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for inclusion in indexed conference proceedings. The publication partner will be announced on this page.

Authors of the best papers will be invited to submit extended versions to special issues of partner journals.

Plagiarism check is applied to all submissions. Similarity above 20% leads to rejection without review.
